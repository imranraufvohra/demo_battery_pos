# How to Sell This App

## 1. Subscription or one-time?
Your website currently promises: **one-time custom build, source code on the client's own GitHub, their own Vercel and Supabase, no lock-in.** Starter from $400, Business + AI from $900, multi-branch after a call.

My recommendation: **keep the one-time build fee, and add an optional monthly care plan.** This matches what the site already says, but gives you recurring income.

| Model | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| One-time only (today) | Simple, matches your promise, easy to sell | No recurring income, every support call is unpaid or ad hoc |
| Pure subscription (you host it on your accounts) | Recurring income | Breaks your "no lock-in / their accounts" promise, you carry hosting cost and all risk |
| **One-time setup + optional monthly care plan (recommended)** | Keeps the promise, recurring revenue, clients keep ownership | You must define the care plan clearly |

### Suggested packages (examples, test and adjust)
- **Setup (one-time):** Starter $400+, Business + AI $900+. Consider testing higher prices for UAE/UK/US buyers. Western buyers often expect more, but check what competitors charge.
- **Care plan (monthly, optional):** backups check, bug fixes, small changes, updates, priority support. Suggested starting range about $25 to $75 per month depending on tier.
- **Pass-through costs:** the client pays Supabase, Vercel and AI usage directly under their own accounts (keeps "no lock-in"). Say this clearly in the quote.
- **Extras (one-time):** extra module, new report, extra branch, data import, staff training.

## 2. What happens when someone orders
1. **Discovery call** (20 to 30 min): products, staff count, tax needs, language, branches.
2. **Quote + contract:** scope, price, timeline, what they own, care plan terms. Take **50% upfront**, balance on delivery.
3. **Build:** new private repo from your template (`pos-core`), new Supabase, new Vercel, their domain, their logo and currency/tax settings, import their stock.
4. **Handover:** repo transferred to their GitHub, accounts owned by them, admin logins, 30-minute training, short user guide.
5. **Support:** 14 to 30 days free bug fixing, then care plan or hourly.

Never reuse one client's data, keys or branding for another client.

## 3. Get leads
1. Ship the live demo (the biggest conversion tool). The demo button leads to `/contact?source=demo&vertical=battery` so you know which leads came from it.
2. A 2-minute screen-recording of: add battery, bill with VAT, warranty claim, ask the AI.
3. Direct outreach to battery, solar, auto-parts and electronics shops in UAE, UK and Pakistan: send the demo link on WhatsApp/LinkedIn plus one sentence about their pain (credit tracking, warranty claims, stock).
4. One case study: "built for a real battery and solar shop" (only with the AK Solar owner's written OK).
5. Freelance platforms and local business groups as a secondary channel.

## 4. Before selling (checklist)
- [ ] Written permission from the AK Solar owner to reuse the code
- [ ] Make `aksolar-app` repo private
- [ ] Simple contract template (IP ownership, payment terms, support scope, liability). **Have a lawyer review it. I am not a lawyer.**
- [ ] Decide how you will receive international payments (check what works for you and the buyer's country, fees, and any local rules)
- [ ] Demo live and tested on phones
- [ ] Clear statement: no tax-compliance claims per country. Real e-invoicing (UAE, UK) is a paid extra
- [ ] Verify current pricing for Supabase, Vercel, Groq before quoting pass-through costs

## 5. Later (after first 2 to 3 clients)
More verticals on the same core (pharmacy, grocery, electronics), Arabic/RTL, per-visitor demo sandbox, then consider a hosted "SaaS" tier as an option for clients who do not want to manage accounts (higher monthly price, you host).
